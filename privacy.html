Privacy Policy

MINImapp (Porto em Família) — Last updated: 9 September 2026

MINImapp ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile-friendly web app that helps families find activities and events for children in the cities it covers. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what stays entirely on your device.

The short version

MINImapp does not have user accounts, does not run any analytics or tracking, and does not collect or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. Everything the app remembers about you — saved events, your child's age filters, your language and city choice — is stored only in your own browser, on your own device, and never leaves it.

Information stored on your device

To make the app usable without an account, it stores a small amount of data locally in your browser (using standard web storage, not a server database):

  • Events you've saved/liked
  • Any child ages you've entered, used only to filter events by age range
  • Your selected city and language
  • Whether you've opted in to see in-app reminder banners for saved events

None of this ever leaves your device — we have no server that receives, stores, or has access to it. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes it. There is no account to delete because none is created.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or any other personally identifiable information. The app does not ask you to log in or create a profile. We do not use cookies for tracking, and we do not run advertising or analytics software of any kind.

Children's privacy

MINImapp is designed to be used by parents and caregivers to find activities for children — it is not designed to be operated directly by children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any child age entered into the app's filters is used solely on-device to narrow down which events are shown, is never transmitted anywhere, and is not linked to any identity.

Third-party services

The app relies on a small number of external services to function:

  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles shown in the app's Map view are loaded from OpenStreetMap's servers. Loading a map tile sends your device's IP address to OpenStreetMap in the same way loading any web image does. See OpenStreetMap's privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — tapping "View on map" for an event opens Google Maps in your browser or the Google Maps app. This is an external link you choose to follow; Google's own privacy policy applies to that page.
  • Event source links — tapping "View source" on an event takes you to that event's own website, which is run by its organiser, not by us.
  • Your email app — the "Suggest an event" button opens your device's own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to gotitasarrumadas@gmail.com. We only see what you choose to send.

In-app purchases (if introduced)

MINImapp may in future offer optional paid unlocks for additional cities through Google Play's own billing system. Any such purchase would be processed entirely by Google Play — we would not receive or store your payment details. This policy will be updated before any such feature goes live, and Google's own privacy policy governs the payment process itself.

Data retention and your choices

Since nothing is stored outside your own device, there is nothing for us to retain or for you to request deletion of. You can clear everything the app remembers at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app, or by uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes — for example, when in-app purchases are introduced — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. We recommend checking back occasionally if you have concerns.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to gotitasarrumadas@gmail.com.

This app is operated by GOTITAS ARRUMADAS, based in Portugal.
